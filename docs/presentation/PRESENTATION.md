## 1. The Current Challenge: The "Leaky Funnel"
Most clinics suffer from a reactive intake process that wastes administrative time and misses high-intent opportunities.

- **Unstructured Data:** Generic forms only capture a name and "interested in surgery."
- **Coordinator Bottleneck:** Staff spends 15–20 minutes per lead gathering basic history that the patient could have provided themselves.
- **High No-Show Rates:** Without a commitment-building intake process, patients are less invested in their scheduled consultations.
- **PHI Risks:** Staff often resort to insecure texting or emailing of patient photos.

## 3. The Solution: A Guided Patient Journey
AestheticAI transforms the intake into a luxury, mobile-first experience.

1. **Visual Selection:** Patient selects their area of interest from a curated menu.
2. **Adaptive Quiz:** Dynamic branching questions gather relevant medical history.
3. **AI-Guided Photos:** Real-time angle overlays ensure clinic-quality photos.
4. **Consent:** HIPAA-compliant biometric and data consent is captured upfront.
5. **AI Analysis:** Face/Body proportions are analyzed automatically in the background.
6. **Lead Delivery:** Your team receives an "Urgent" alert with a full clinical brief.

---

## 4. AI Computer Vision Suite
Every lead arrives with a full clinical picture, significantly reducing the "discovery" phase of the consultation.

- **Golden Ratio Analysis:** Automated measurements of facial thirds, fifths, and nasal width.
- **Symmetry Scoring:** Evaluation of nasal deviation, eye symmetry, and overall harmony.
- **Quality Control:** Fuzzy or misaligned photos are caught by the AI before the patient submits, ensuring your team has usable data.
- **Procedure Recommendation:** The system matches patient anatomy to the procedures your clinic performs.

---

## 5. Lead Intelligence & Scoring
AestheticAI doesn't just collect leads; it ranks them so your sales team knows exactly who to call first.

**Priority Tiers:**
- **🔴 Urgent (80–100):** High budget, immediate timeline, excellent clinical candidate.
- **🟡 High (60–79):** Realistic timeline, strong intent, good anatomical match.
- **🟢 Medium (40–59):** Early research phase, nurture candidate.
- **⚪ Standard (<40):** Informational interest only.

**Score Components:** Timeline (30%), Budget alignment (25%), AI Harmony Score (20%), Photo Quality (10%), Concern Depth (10%), Referral Source (5%).
