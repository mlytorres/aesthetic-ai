# Affiliate Partner Guide

Last updated: April 18, 2026

## Who this is for
Creators/influencers invited by a clinic to participate in the AestheticAI affiliate program.

## How to register
1. Receive your secure Partner Portal URL from the clinic.
2. Open the URL and confirm your profile information.
3. Accept the current Partner Terms version.
4. Review your approved campaign links before posting.

## Posting rules
- Use only approved creatives listed in your portal.
- Include required ad disclosures based on campaign guidance.
- Do not edit tracking URLs.
- Do not publish unapproved medical claims.

## How attribution works
A referral is tracked through these events:
1. Click on your tracking URL.
2. Intake started.
3. Evaluation completed.

Payout eligibility requires:
- Active partner status
- Current terms accepted
- Active campaign
- Approved linked creative asset

## Payout status definitions
- `pending_hold`: Qualified evaluation recorded; waiting hold window.
- `approved`: Clinic approved payout.
- `rejected`: Clinic rejected payout with reason.
- `released`: Clinic released payout.

## What data you can see
Your portal shows only aggregated metrics:
- Clicks
- Intake starts
- Completed evaluations
- Pending payout total
- Released payout total

Patient-identifying data is not shown.

## Troubleshooting
- If your link gets no credit: verify terms are accepted and link status is active.
- If payouts are not released: check hold window and clinic review status.
- If your portal URL stops working: request a new portal link from clinic admin.

## Support
Contact your clinic affiliate manager for campaign approvals, disputes, and payout questions.
