# Simulations & consultations

_Last verified: 2026-06-12_

Two tools that turn an interested patient into a committed one: AI result previews and built-in video consultations.

## AI simulations

From an evaluation, staff (owner/admin/coordinator/surgeon) can generate an **AI simulation** — a preview of potential results based on the patient's own photos.

1. Open the evaluation → **Simulation** → generate.
2. Review the result before sharing — you are the quality gate.
3. Share via the **secure share link**; the patient opens it without logging in (the link token is the credential).

Patients who see a simulation of their own anatomy book consultations at a much higher rate — use it for hesitant high-value leads.

## Video consultations

Schedule and run video consultations without leaving the platform:

1. Open the evaluation → **Consultations** → schedule (owner/admin/coordinator).
2. The patient receives a **join link** — no account or app required.
3. Staff join from the consultation page; surgeons can join any consultation on their evaluations.
4. Cancel from the same place if plans change (the patient link stops working).

## Tips

- Generate the simulation **before** the consultation and walk through it live — it anchors the conversation.
- Share links are tokenized URLs; treat them like passwords and don't post them anywhere public.
- If a patient can't join, send the link again by email/text — it's the same URL.
