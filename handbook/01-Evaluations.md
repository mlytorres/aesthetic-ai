# Evaluations

_Last verified: 2026-06-12_

The evaluations queue is where pre-qualified patient profiles land and where your team works them to a booked consultation.

## The queue

**Evaluations** in the sidebar. Each row shows procedure, status, priority, lead score, and when it arrived. Filter and sort to work the hottest leads first.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Submitted / analyzing** | Patient finished intake; AI is processing |
| **Complete** | Analysis done — ready for review |
| **Contacted** | Your team has reached out |
| **Booked** | Consultation scheduled |

Update the status as you work — it drives the dashboard counts and analytics funnel.

## Inside an evaluation

- **Patient & contact info** — who they are and how to reach them
- **Quiz answers** — procedure-specific responses, including safety questions
- **Photos** — the guided captures from intake
- **AI analysis** — proportion/symmetry findings and suggested procedures
- **Lead score & priority** — urgency hint for triage
- **Notes** — your team's running log (use it; it's how handoffs survive)

## Clinical brief

**Download brief** produces the surgeon-ready PDF summary: photos, quiz answers, AI findings. Owners, admins, coordinators, and surgeons can download it — viewers cannot. Send it to the surgeon before the consultation; it typically saves 15–20 minutes per consult.

## CSV export

Coordinators and above can export the queue to CSV (for reporting or CRM import). Exports contain PHI — handle per clinic policy.

## Tips

- Work **urgent** priority first, then newest **complete**.
- Always log contact attempts in **Notes** — analytics and teammates depend on it.
- If the evaluation came from an affiliate link, attribution is recorded automatically — nothing to do manually.
