# Analytics

_Last verified: 2026-06-12_

**Analytics** (all roles except surgeon) shows how your intake funnel performs.

## What you'll find

- **Funnel** — widget opens → quiz started → photos → submitted → contacted → booked, with drop-off at each step
- **Volume over time** — evaluations per day/week
- **Procedure mix** — which procedures drive inquiries
- **Lead quality** — score distribution and conversion by score band

## Reading the funnel

| Symptom | Usual cause |
|---------|-------------|
| Big drop at quiz | Too many questions for that procedure — review the quiz |
| Big drop at photos | Patients on desktop or poor guidance — push the mobile link |
| Submitted but never contacted | Team response time — set a same-day SLA |
| Contacted but not booked | Script/offer issue, or slow follow-up cadence |

## Tips

- Watch **time-to-first-contact** above all; it's the strongest predictor of booking.
- Compare procedures before judging the whole funnel — one bad quiz can drag the average down.
