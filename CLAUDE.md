Project Document: Aesthetic AI Pre-Evaluation SaaS Platform
1. Executive Summary
The platform is a "Digital Consultation Concierge" designed to bridge the gap between a basic lead inquiry and a high-intent surgical patient. By using AI-driven anatomical analysis and interactive quizzes, it provides clinics with "pre-qualified clinical profiles" rather than just contact information.
2. Core Product Modules
The platform will mirror the Chapter-based architecture of a comprehensive CRM but focus specifically on the "Phase 1: Lead Acquisition" stage.
A. The "Smart Intake" Engine
Anatomical 3D Mapping: Instead of text fields, patients interact with 3D models to "drop pins" on areas of concern, such as the nasal tip or jawline.
Dynamic Branching Quiz: Questions adapt based on the procedure selected (e.g., asking about BMI for a BBL or skin laxity for a facelift).
B. AI Computer Vision Suite (Build vs. Buy)
Recommendation: Build the core logic but leverage medical-grade APIs for foundational processing.
Ghosting/Transparency Overlay: A mobile-optimized tool that ensures patients take "Before" photos at perfect angles for the surgeon's review.
Proportion Analysis: AI calculates symmetry and "Golden Ratio" metrics to provide the patient with a "Beauty Roadmap."
Anatomic Matching: Automatically suggests procedures (e.g., "Deep Plane Facelift" vs. "Dermal Fillers") based on visual skin laxity and age.
C. The Clinic Dashboard (Multi-Tenant)
Lead Scoring & Priority Queues: High-value inquiries (like facelifts) are automatically flagged and placed at the top of the coordinator's call list.
Secure Magic Links: To maintain 100% HIPAA compliance, patient data and photos are never sent via standard email. Instead, the clinic receives a "New Evaluation Ready" notification with a secure link to the encrypted portal.
3. Technical Architecture & Security
Modern Tech Stack: Follow your successful Miami Life CRM stack: Laravel (Backend), React/Inertia (Frontend), and TailwindCSS for a premium, luxury UI.
Infrastructure: Use AWS HealthLake or Google Cloud Healthcare API to ensure data is stored in a pre-certified HIPAA environment.
Compliance: You must sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every clinic to legally protect their patient data.
4. Market Strategy for Potential Customers
Your potential leads in Miami (Miami Life, 305 Plastic Surgery, etc.) already handle high volumes of surgical procedures, such as Abdomen J-Plasma and Lipo 360.
Value Proposition for Clinics:
Speed-to-Lead AI: Qualifies leads 24/7 and offers instant consultation booking.
Reduced "No-Shows": Patients who engage with interactive AI tools and see 3D simulations are significantly more likely to commit to a paid consultation.
Operational Efficiency: Surgeons receive a "pre-op clinical brief" before they ever meet the patient, saving 15-20 minutes per consult.
5. Development Roadmap
MVP Phase: Focus on the "AI-Guided Photo Capture" and "Secure Intake Portal" for one core procedure (e.g., Rhinoplasty or Liposuction).
SaaS Integration: Build webhooks to sync leads directly into major CRMs like Nextech or PatientNow.
Expansion: Add AI simulations that show "potential results" based on the patient's own photos.
